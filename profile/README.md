## About Us

Pajoohan Sepand is a technology company with extensive experience in building software solutions for the transportation industry.

Our flagship product, Payaneh, is a digital transportation platform designed to simplify ticketing and travel experiences.

Our engineering team focuses on solving real-world challenges through modern software architecture, reliable infrastructure, and scalable technology.
